# Content #005 — "It's Somewhere in the Inbox" — Social Copy

## Instagram / TikTok
"Do we have the signed agreement?"
"I think Sarah emailed it."

The information exists. It's just buried in threads, forwards, and attachments — 34 results, 3 versions, and no way to tell which one is real.

The problem isn't missing information. It's missing structure.

## LinkedIn
Every business has some version of this exchange: "Do we have that?" — "I think it's in an email somewhere."

The information almost always exists. It's just trapped inside inboxes, forwarded threads, attachments, and individual memory. So the search begins — new query, new keyword, another attachment that might be the right version. Searching harder was never the fix.

The real issue is that nothing gives the information structure. Captured once and tied to the right customer or project — versioned, and findable where the work actually happens — it stops being a scavenger hunt and starts being something the business can rely on.

Your business should know what your business knows. Your inbox shouldn't be your operating system.
