# Content #004 — "Waiting for Approval" — Social Copy

## Instagram / TikTok
The work was finished days ago. It's just… waiting for someone to approve it.

It sits in an inbox. Then a chat. Then a "did you see this?" No one's sure who has it or what happens after.

That's not a people problem. The workflow simply has no way to move it forward.

When something's ready, the next step should already know who owns it.

## LinkedIn
Most "slow" businesses aren't slow at doing the work. They're slow at moving it.

A proposal is finished — then it stops. It waits in an inbox, a chat thread, a follow-up nobody's sure they should send. Meanwhile, no one can say who owns the approval, what exactly needs signing off, or what's supposed to happen next.

That's not a discipline problem. It's a missing workflow.

Good systems make approval explicit: who approves, what they're approving, when it's due, and what gets triggered the moment it's done. The work keeps moving because the path is designed, not remembered.

When something's ready, the next step should already know.
